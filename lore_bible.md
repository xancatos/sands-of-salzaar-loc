# DesertLegend (Sands of Salzaar) Lore Bible

## Game Identity
- **Original Chinese Name**: 部落与弯刀 (Tribes and Scimitars)
- **Official English Title**: Sands of Salzaar
- **Setting**: A dark fantasy / ancient desert RPG. The tone should remain serious, ancient, and immersive. Avoid overly modern slang.

## Factions & Groups
- **Development Team**: Sands of Salzaar development team

## System Terminology
- **Zen Mode** (禅道模式): Hides all non-essential UI elements for maximum immersion.
- **New Game** (新游戏): New Game
- **Load Game** (读取游戏): Load Game
